An AI news desk for your Telegram channel. It watches public channels on your
list, drops repeats of news you already have, drafts posts in your channel's
voice and publishes them only after you press Publish in your Telegram bot.

- One news hook, one proposal: follow-ups about something you already published
  or rejected are not proposed again, and each repeat says what it repeats.
- Every draft arrives in the bot exactly as it will be published, with Publish,
  Reject, Another variant and Edit buttons. After a decision only a one-line
  outcome stays in the chat.
- Forward any post to the bot to have it drafted out of turn.
- The admin page shows the queue, sources, history, the filtered list, settings
  and status.
- Models run on the subscriptions connected to bb, or on any OpenAI-compatible API.

The same service also runs as a standalone script with the same config file.
Source and documentation: https://github.com/AiXenix/AiXenix-Telegram-Scraper-AI
